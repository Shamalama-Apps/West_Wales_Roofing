A sagging roof is almost always a timber problem rather than a covering problem, and this one was no different. The felt, battens and tiles came off completely and the broken rafters were doubled up with new four by two so the roof had something straight to sit on again.

It went back on new membrane and battens, finished in Marley Modern tile. Three weeks in total including the scaffold going up and coming down.
