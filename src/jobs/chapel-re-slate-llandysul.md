The original slate was beyond salvage on the south pitch and the valleys had been repaired in mortar. We stripped both pitches, replaced twelve rafter ends, re-felted and battened, then re-slated in new Welsh slate with lead valleys and a new ridge. The chapel stayed in use throughout, so the scaffold was sheeted and the site swept every evening.
