The slate itself was sound. What had failed was the felt beneath it, which is usually the case on a roof of this age, so replacing the covering would have thrown away perfectly good slate for no reason.

The roof was stripped, the perished felt replaced, and the original slate relaid with only the broken ones swapped out. Two weeks in total, including work around the chimney that is not in these photographs.
